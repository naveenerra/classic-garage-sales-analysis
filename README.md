# Classic Garage Sales Analysis Dashboard
Excel-based sales analytics dashboard analyzing transactions, sales, profit, customers, employees, product categories, and monthly/weekly sales trends.


## 📊 Project Overview

An Excel-based Sales Analytics Dashboard developed to analyze Classic Garage's sales performance, profitability, customer contribution, employee performance, product categories, and sales trends.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into an interactive business intelligence report using Microsoft Excel.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze sales data and answer key business questions:

- How are sales performing over time?
- Which product categories generate the most sales?
- Which customers contribute the most revenue?
- Which employees generate the highest sales?
- How do weekly and monthly sales trends change?
- What are the major sales patterns in the business?

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### Sales Trend Analysis

The dashboard analyzes total sales by month and week to identify changes in sales performance throughout the year.

### Product Category Analysis

Sales are distributed across:

- Classic Cars
- Vintage Cars
- Motorcycles
- Others

### Customer Analysis

The dashboard identifies the top customers based on sales contribution.

### Employee Analysis

The dashboard highlights the top-performing employees based on total sales.

### Year Filter

An interactive year slicer allows users to analyze different years.

## 🔍 Key Insights

Some of the major observations from the dashboard include:

- Classic Cars represent the largest share of sales among the displayed categories.
- Sales vary significantly across months.
- November shows a major increase in monthly sales.
- A small number of customers contribute a significant portion of total sales.
- Employee sales performance varies considerably.
- Weekly sales show several periods of significant spikes.
